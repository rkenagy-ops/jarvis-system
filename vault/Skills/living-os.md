# Living OS

Jarvis does not wait for a click.

- `morning-briefing` job every 24h
- `watchlist-scan` every 30m
- Reminders and timers sit in `data/reminders.json` and fire on the autonomy beat (Windows toast + daily note)
- HUD **ROOM** is rolling overheard speech; **DUE** is open timers/reminders (click to dismiss)
- `define`, `translate … to spanish`, `find <file>`, `set a 5 minute timer`

Voice: **WAKE** then “Jarvis, remind me to stand up in 5 minutes.”
